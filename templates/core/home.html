How a viewing works

  1. We check the home first. Our team inspects every listing and confirms the owner's permission, the rent, the location and that it's available. Each listing shows what was checked and when.
  2. You request a time. Nothing to pay yet.
  3. We confirm it. We arrange access with the property and a broker, then text you the confirmed time and the viewing fee.
  4. You pay to secure the slot. Your booking is confirmed only once the payment provider confirms your payment.
  5. A broker meets you there and guides the viewing. Afterwards we follow up and can suggest similar verified homes.

Verification means our team checked the details listed on the date shown. It isn't a legal guarantee. If anything is wrong, tell us and we'll act on it.
